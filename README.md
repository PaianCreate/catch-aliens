# Catch Aliens 🛸

A pixel-art neck-stretch break for desk workers. Steer a UFO with your head (webcam + MediaPipe face tracking), hold gentle neck stretches to beam up alien cats, space cows and cosmic chicks, and grab as many as you can in the 10-second ALIEN RUSH.

**Play:** https://paiancreate.github.io/catch-aliens/

- Camera frames are analyzed on your device only. Nothing is uploaded.
- Test shortcuts: `?sim=1` (keyboard instead of camera), `?final` (jump to the Final Rush), `?gold` (golden creatures).
